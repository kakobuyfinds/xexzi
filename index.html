From shortlist to checkout

How to use this spreadsheet guide

A good find starts with a clear listing and ends with a realistic total cost.

01 / DISCOVER

Make a focused shortlist

Browse the featured products by category and note the item ID. Compare similar items by measurements, materials and intended use rather than by the headline price alone.

02 / VERIFY

Check the original listing

Open HipobuyFind, locate the item and inspect its available seller details. Confirm the exact size, color, quantity and current price. Check whether your chosen agent accepts the original product link.

03 / REVIEW

Confirm before shipping

If your order includes warehouse inspection, review your own QC photos and request useful measurements. Confirm the shipping quote, destination restrictions and any return deadline before releasing the parcel.

Inspect the details

What to check in QC photos

Seller photos show a listing. Warehouse QC photos, when provided, show an inspected order. Neither is an automatic guarantee of authenticity, durability or fit.

  • Identity: correct item, color, size label and number of pieces.
  • Condition: stains, holes, loose stitching, damaged closures or uneven finishing.
  • Measurements: compare requested measurements with clothing you already own.
  • Completeness: check pairs, straps and accessories included in the order.
  • Missing views: request close-ups where a detail is hidden or blurred.
Before you pay

Keep a useful comparison record

Record the source URL, item ID, chosen variant, date checked, item price and estimated delivered cost. Add measurements and your own inspection notes when available.

A listing can remain online while a particular size is unavailable. A low price may refer to an accessory, deposit or different option. Confirm the selected variant rather than relying on a catalog card.

This directory does not verify seller authorization or product authenticity. Source titles and photographs identify listings; they are not endorsements.

Good questions, clear answers

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a product research list used to organize finds, listing links, reference prices and comparison notes. A listing still needs to be checked for availability and compatibility with your chosen buying agent.

Where do the products on this page come from?

The six featured listings are a snapshot of the HipobuyFind catalog checked on 19 November 2026. The OrientDig spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons open HipobuyFind. This page is an independent guide, not the official OrientDig website.

Are the displayed prices the total delivered cost?

No. They are reference item prices from the source catalog. Domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, exchange costs and applicable taxes or duties may add to the final total.

Are these product images QC photos?

The images are source catalog listing images. They are not presented as verified warehouse QC photos. Review inspection images for your own order when available.

Can every listing be purchased through OrientDig?

Compatibility has not been verified. Locate the original seller link, check it with your chosen agent and follow its current purchasing restrictions. A product appearing in a directory does not establish that an agent can purchase or ship it.

How do I find a featured item after opening HipobuyFind?

Use the item ID printed on the card, such as 154917 for the fleece-lined hoodie. Search the source catalog or locate the corresponding item page. Recheck all details because listings and prices may change.

Does this page refresh prices and stock automatically?

No. It contains a dated selection of real catalog listings. Search and sorting operate on those six entries. Visit HipobuyFind for the current catalog and confirm availability before ordering.
